## Useful distinctions already extracted

CSPIT's lift table was checked visually so the No ticks are not mistaken for Yes after text extraction. Its79cm opening,205x133cm cabin and inaccessible approach are reported observations, not observations independently measured by us. The title metadata and a passage retain different institute names (ARIP/RPCP); the CSPIT cover/building name is recorded alongside that discrepancy.

HUD PDFpp37--40 (printed27--30) distinguish manufacturer/device prices, shipping and willingness to pay. Product figures are approximately USD1150 StairSteady,2060 AssiStep,10400 FlexStep and1500 All-Push Door; shipping is excluded and installation labor is not included. They are historical context, not prices for the Indian audits or our Clinic/Molio cases. The FlexStep figure concerns four steps/about3ft, not an arbitrary full-storey connection. The human evaluation used a demonstration platform/doorway mock-up (PDFp23), so it does not validate our optimization outputs. Stair-assist devices do not automatically serve a wheelchair profile. Exhibit5's floor plans are illustrative and have not been admitted as metric source geometry.

Dolton's four priority-group allowances are not separate per-action prices. Its missing appendices prevent checking the advertised detailed item list and ground-floor plan. The supplied introduction's2007 copyright/content is distinguished from its stale PDF title metadata. Raw source files and images are not redistributed in Git.

A first-floor total differs between summary p10 and header p61. Metric extraction and reuse rights remain unverified.

This is the strongest linked case candidate so far. Admission is deliberately unresolved: adding a lobby may change frozen geometry/topology, which cannot be silently accommodated by changing the research problem. Only actions fitting the existing model can enter a recognized benchmark; unsupported renovations remain documented exclusions. A PDF reference drawing cannot be assumed to provide exact room polygons, elevations or permitted collision footprints.
