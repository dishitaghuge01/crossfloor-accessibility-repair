proof and controlled optimization experiments. Historical price extraction,
source-access/admission studies and the decision-simple CAD pilot are retained
in a separate evidence report and the private review package's
supplementary-evidence/ subtree. They are not evidence of difficult practical
access-network optimization or source-authenticated construction repairs.
