# Frozen frontier/scaling study

22 October 2026. Follow-up within the fixed physical-repair direction. Prior
primary failures and all existing records remain unchanged. Authored families
are development-guided, not a blind holdout or a random building population.

## Bounded local bands

Sizes8,24,48 bands, price seeds0,1,2: nine price variants of three catalogue
topologies. Each band spans two consecutive transitions. All room geometry is
frozen; two independent profiles have distinct terminal demands. Catalogue choices
include a shared S shaft with individual segments and an atomic two-edge bundle,
overlapping competing Q purchases for profile0, a separate U bundle for profile1,
and an alternative shared T bundle. Adjacent bands' different owners compete at
their common landing floor. Bands cannot be optimized independently. The selected
Q/U pair is a genuine alternative to a shared-profile purchase, not duplicate
profile obligations. Costs are positive seeded abstract integers.

Two methods: uncompressed both-projection DP and the existing strengthened single
worker CP-SAT model. Two fresh-process repetitions in forward/reverse order per
input,36 observations,90s external/85s internal budgets,100000 raw-state and65536
cut-column caps, no RAM cap. Report cost-stage proof separately from full exact
objective, completed pipeline times separately from limits, recognition/optimization/
replay, and worker peak memory. No source edits/commits during execution.

Static potential frontier certificates are generated independently of observed
states. Expected family values, checked before freezing, are b<=8, at most five
potential cells, product domain<=108 and L=3. These bounds characterize the
authored family, not the unrelated building-derived inputs. At small development
instances, exhaustive enumeration agrees on the complete objective and inter-band
conflicts are verified. A scaling advantage is a result only if the frozen runs
support it; no speed/completion target is a stopping rule.

## Private-bay separating family

Sizes2,8,16,32 bays on three floors. Same-state-space comparison of floor DP,
both-projection DP and CP-SAT, two counterbalanced repetitions each:24 observations,
same90s/85s budgets/caps. One fixed positive cost pattern, one profile. Its purpose
is the certified bound separation; it is not a difficult application case. The
analytic unique cost/count/ID optimum is the first bay's cost-three bundle.

## Development evidence, exclusions and validation boundary

Six-action-per-band development probes were replaced before the final freeze
because profile-restricted choices did not participate in the selected repair.
The final eight-action-per-band catalogue has separate Q/U profile choices, which
do participate in the development optimum. Raw pilot files under
`runs/local-band*-pilot*` are excluded from research totals. They guided size/budget
selection and are not confirmatory observations. Both pilot configurations and
unfavorable outcomes remain preserved locally.

Neither family is extracted from a real building. Reusable generators, canonical
JSON, source/seed/geometry/action/conflict metadata, static certificates, replayed
repairs and machine-readable outcomes form a controlled intervention benchmark.
No license grant for third-party source material is inferred. Actual construction
validation still needs authenticated reservations/permissions/intervention data and
domain review. The computational/theoretical follow-up cannot substitute for that.
