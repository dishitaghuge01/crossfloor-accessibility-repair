## Why the two evidence gates remain open

A decisive result over applicable general decomposition would require a proved distinction that those methods do not already obtain under equivalent encoding/scheduling assumptions. Our Bell/domain-product counting and eight-mask recurrence can be recovered by standard finite-domain/frontier methods. The complete physical-action construction and safe projection proof are supported contributions, but neither is a demonstrated generic complexity separation. More benchmarks cannot convert that equivalence into a new theorem, and a bounded literature check cannot prove absence of every antecedent.

Verified actual-building intervention validation requires authenticated available reservations, candidate installation/ownership and access-policy evidence, constructibility review of the proposed complete effects, and reliable comparative costs if monetary benefit is claimed. Current source geometry and permission to use drawings establish neither those facts nor qualified site acceptance. We can provide hypothetical controlled annotations and a reusable benchmark, but cannot manufacture site/design verification or label assumptions as observations. No additional user data is required to complete this controlled package; these stronger claims remain unsupported.
