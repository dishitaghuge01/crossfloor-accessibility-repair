# Building-network and event-lifetime results

This is new experimental work, not a relabeling of the earlier evidence.
The original 2274 main observations and 12 CAD observations stay frozen.

## What changed

Optional last-use cell/vertex forgetting uses the existing owned-interface
DP. Cells expire after their last complete occupying unit; profile vertices
expire after their last incident action/baseline event. Physical ownership
still remains live throughout each complete unit, all paid effects remain,
and vanished unanchored components still reject the state. Both flags default
off. A compilation-local geometry cache is shared by every current method.
The worst-case theorem and priority qualification are unchanged.

The final code passes 765 regression tests in 72.93s. New tests compare exact
labels with exhaustive search and cached/uncached geometry outcomes.

## Workload and independence units

The clinic uses 253 retained original room projections across three original
storeys. Molio uses 93 across eight. Five catalogue layouts, with three abstract
price seeds each, produce 15 controlled inputs from TWO buildings. Do not
count them as 15 buildings. The fixed 70-observation campaign has rotated
six-method ordering (without reversing the primary pair), fresh processes, 30-second parent deadlines and explicit
repetition roles. All manifests report clean revision f1e5d188738e53cdfe9f3532d036398e97e1d278. See protocol
and source/hash records in the package.

Unlike the earlier two/three-action catalogues, the largest clinic input
has 190 purchases, 103 destinations, 47 bundled purchases, 102 active components
after baseline contraction and 22 independent cycles. Molio's potential active
graph is sparser; eight source storeys do not by themselves make it hard.
Two route-profile layers have identical permissions/demands here, and the
separated grid does not create competing-owner overlaps. These limitations
are explicit; semantic/owner-conflict tests remain separate evidence.

All baseline door annotations, profiles, reservations, connector designs,
permissions and costs are hypothetical assumptions. Original room geometry
and source identity are retained; verified construction data are not supplied.
This is building-derived controlled network evaluation, not validated actual
building repairs.

## Measured outcomes

Across the 15 price variants, lifetime DP completes 6 and CP-SAT completes 8. On seed 0, lifetime DP completes 2 of the five layouts whose original DP controls fail. Its median pipeline time is lower on 1/1 layouts with three optimal repetitions for both methods. Layouts with three lifetime-DP optima and no CP-SAT optimal pipeline completion under the same deadline: 1. These are descriptive workstation results, not a general runtime superiority claim.

All 15 inputs additionally have explicit, replay-checked nonoptimal segment
controls. Seed 0 costs are 342 for the 78-action clinic (exact DP optimum 267),
726 for the 190-action clinic, and 198 for the 152-action Molio case. These
checks establish feasibility under the declared model, not optimality or
construction validation; they are not extra timed solver observations.

Every returned selection is checked for paid rectangular overlap, original
atomic cost, required reachability and delta_A=0. Source room rings/IDs are
checked against the frozen inventories. Exact labels agree wherever more
than one method completes. Large results are not exhaustively certified.

CP-SAT's exact count/ID tie stages can be expensive. The manuscript separately
reports cost-stage and tie-stage search time. A faster complete pipeline or
a deadline win must not be represented as superiority at minimum-cost
optimization. Single-run controls and price checks are labeled separately
from three-repetition timing comparisons. Failures are retained in full.

## What this does and does not settle

The implementation now has a demonstrated completion-range improvement over
its original DP, and evaluation includes substantially larger active networks.
General superiority over CP-SAT, representative real-building performance,
verified physical interventions and literature priority are not established.
The earlier 15 unfavorable connectivity timings remain unchanged; weighted
rank reduction is not credited for the new event-forgetting improvement.
The incomplete earlier clean campaign is retained as preliminary, without
splicing its timings into the final 70 observations.
