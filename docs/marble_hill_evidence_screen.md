Inspected 16 October 2026. Documentary lead only; zero admitted solver
instances. No raw source files, source artwork or source text redistributed.

## Primary sources and inspection limits

1. [Architect design/access report, revision C, 24 August 2018](https://www.english-heritage.org.uk/link/722a3f30b0374c3eb903129da002158b.aspx), nine PDF pages. Text inspected; no metric geometry extraction or visual drawing audit performed.
2. [September 2018 planning statement](https://www.english-heritage.org.uk/link/5c94c36216184066b9685eb276b9d1e1.aspx), 41 PDF pages. Relevant proposal text and drawing schedule inspected, not every appendix manually reviewed.
3. [Owner project journal](https://www.english-heritage.org.uk/visit/places/marble-hill/marble-hill-revived/), December 2018 and February 2022 entries inspected.

The report places a ground-to-first-floor platform lift in an existing former
closet/control room (PDF pp.5,8-9), describes partial floor/ceiling removal,
and gives staff-managed step-free entrance arrangements. The planning
schedule (PDF p.37) identifies drawings 300 A (existing lift area), 301 E
(proposed), and 302 B (cladding). The journal reports planning approval in
December 2018 and lift installation by February 2022. These are owner and
architect documentary statements, not our site inspection or certification.
