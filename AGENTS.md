# Working rules

- Never claim novelty, acceptance, or a theorem before direct evidence supports it.
- Keep strict and relaxed repair problems separate.
- Keep representation corrections and physical interventions separate.
- Do not commit source BIM files, licensed datasets, raw PDFs, secrets, or generated experiment artifacts.
- Commit reviewable milestones. Do not push automatically.
- Every experiment needs a manifest, seed, input fingerprints, environment, and solver status.
- Update docs/master_plan.md and PROGRESS.md whenever a research gate changes.
