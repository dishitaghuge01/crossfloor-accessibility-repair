\subsection{Same-Model Comparators and Evidence}
The independent CP-SAT formulation~\cite{cpsat} uses original-action Booleans $x_a$ and owner/cell variables $y_{oc}=\bigvee_{a:(o,c)\in U_a}x_a$, with $\sum_o y_{oc}\le1$ per cell. Every paid footprint belongs to $U_a$. For each profile, integral flow sends one unit from its root to each distinct nonroot target. Directed actual-room edge capacities are the target count times enablement: one for baseline edges, the OR of supplier actions for candidate edges. Summing conservation over any unrooted component excludes targets there; valid paths conversely supply integral flow. Purchase vertices never become route connectors.

Costs are exactly integer-scaled within a checked safe range. OR-Tools 9.14.6206 uses one worker and seed zero. Sequential solves fix minimum cost, count and ascending-ID membership decisions; every stage must be OPTIMAL for an exact full label. Interruptions retain available replayed incumbents and cost bounds. Relative cost gap is $\max(0,c-b)/|c|$ for nonzero incumbent $c$ and bound $b$; zero with zero bound has gap zero, otherwise unavailable cases are null. Cost optimality during an interrupted tie stage does not establish full-label optimality. Interrupted first-stage bounds outside exact double-integer range are unavailable.

Whole-action greedy uses cost per newly reached profile--room pair. Its improved variant prunes redundant actions, then deterministically first-improves exchanges removing at most two selected actions and adding one unselected action, with 100 fully replayed proposals. Incompatible proposals count against the budget. Pruning follows each accepted exchange; accepted cost/count/ID labels strictly improve. Failure remains heuristic failure; local exhaustion supplies neither completeness nor an approximation guarantee. Construction-only greedy remains separately labelled.

All returned selections receive an additional full topology/physical/profile replay. Small oracle certificates also use separate materialization, union-find and subset enumeration, sharing schema/geometry predicates. Independent box arithmetic rechecks paid rectangle overlap and cost without Shapely or solver calls. Translation, input permutation, order-preserving identifier changes, geometry/profile scaling away from tolerance boundaries, exactly representable cost rescaling, dominated-action addition, feasible-candidate monotonicity and cache-fingerprint tests supplement solver agreement. Arbitrary identifier permutations may change the declared lexicographic tie choice. Large analytical inputs are not labelled exhaustive certificates.

Compression on/off, safe marked-component pruning and cached recognition are valid ablations. Cache preparation is excluded only in separately labelled reuse observations; it is never subtracted from primary end-to-end timing. Dominance disabling and alternate scheduling were not implemented, and no unmeasured benefit is claimed. Discarded paid geometry, forbidden compatible reuse and split bundle prices are altered-model counterexamples, not valid competing algorithms.

\section{Discussion and Limitations}
The supported advance is the specialized physical-to-owned-interface construction and its exactness/complexity transfer, supplemented by reproducible evaluation. Labelled connectivity, condition-specific design and weighted representative bases are prior ideas. General formulations can encode the finite model. The explicit generic upper-bound comparison does not establish a universal parameter separation, and an equivalent prior construction may exist.

Finite tests cannot replace independent proof review. Independent box checks cover paid rectangular overlap/cost, not every clearance predicate or source-evidence assumption. The certificate verifier still shares geometry/schema. Tiny arrangements, numerical tolerance and operational caps remain limitations. Arbitrary demands, candidate doors, dependencies, exclusions, reshaping and metadata-only corrections remain unsupported. Supplied abstract costs are not estimates of construction expenditure. Exact objective labels refer to the decimal values of decoded JSON numeric representations; the current loader does not preserve arbitrarily long lexical decimal literals beyond its numeric parser precision. The reported integer/quarter-cost experiments avoid that issue.

The richer controlled source experiments have multi-room adjacency, horizontally dependent intervention choices and consecutive three-storey routes, but admit only two source models. Other screening outcomes remain visible. Hypothetical baseline doors and permissions are not source facts. Conservative projected background may overreject locations. These results establish neither authenticated building restoration, statutory accessibility nor whole-building constructibility. State/recognition bottlenecks and unfavorable compression outcomes remain part of the result.

\section{Conclusion}
Exact strict repair can preserve frozen rooms and adjacency, original atomic prices and complete physical ownership while compressing profile-specific route witnesses. The measured interface identifies the restrictions enabling the bound; two-floor hardness shows why floor count alone is insufficient. Same-model optimization, seeded stress, valid ablations, semantic counterexamples and multi-room source-derived experiments make the construction and its limits inspectable.
