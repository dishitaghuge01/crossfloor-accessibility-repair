\title{Exact Topology-Preserving Cross-Floor Accessibility Repair with Shared Physical Assets}
\author{}
\begin{document}
\maketitle
\begin{abstract}
Accessibility repairs in multi-floor buildings must distinguish traversable access from room adjacency and preserve physical purchase semantics. One installation action may supply several profile-specific connections, share an asset with other actions, and occupy space unrelated to a selected route. We study exact repair with frozen room identities, polygons and adjacency under a rooted, fixed-template activation model. Our method compiles rectangular collisions into owned cells and processes complete action--asset coupling units in a floor-interface dynamic program. Complete original costs and physical effects remain materialized while established rank-based machinery compresses connectivity witnesses. We derive a conditional interface bound governed by profiles, baseline components, collision cells, complete support span and logarithmic owner multiplicity. Evaluation includes 37 authored cases, eight controlled variants derived from one licensed building model, and four explicit semantic simplifications. Completed comparable solvers agree on exact costs and original action identifiers; small instances have separately replayed exhaustive certificates. Larger analytical families retain small state sets, but recognition costs and operational limits remain visible. Compression gains are modest. Building-derived profiles, reservations and prices are hypothetical assumptions. The contribution is a specialized ownership and full-action accounting transfer with reproducible evidence; literature priority and general construction validity are not established.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Building information models distinguish physical space, openings and connectivity, yet a repair decision requires more than finding a missing graph edge. A shared wall need not admit traversal; an existing connector need not have permission for every accessibility profile. A proposed installation can affect multiple floors and destinations for one price, overlap another installation, or share a physical facility with a compatible action. These distinctions matter when room topology is frozen rather than regenerated.

Consider Fig.~\ref{fig:demo}: two profiles need to reach two upstairs destinations. A baseline lift reaches circulation room C for both profiles, while an existing door connects C to T0 for only profile zero. Only one of four obligations initially holds. One original action purchases two new lift effects for three abstract cost units, enabling every obligation without changing room adjacency. Separate effects cost four. A graph-only cheapest repair need not be physically compatible, and independently pricing effects changes the optimization problem.

\subsection{Research Question and Contributions}
We ask: \emph{Can exact rooted accessibility repair preserve complete physical actions and shared ownership while keeping exponential state dependence on a measured floor interface rather than raw catalogue identities?} The scope is precise: independent physical portal activations, rectangular fixed templates, nonnegative costs, separate rooted profile layers, and a reserved-interface two-dimensional kernel.

The paper develops three connected contributions:
\begin{enumerate}
\item An owned-cell representation and complete coupling-unit sweep preserving action-specific occupancy, verified same-owner reuse and original cost/count/identifier ordering.
\item A full-action/thin-witness correctness argument and transfer of established weighted representative partitions to the measured interface, yielding the conditional bound in \eqref{eq:bound}.
\item A manifest-driven evaluation: small exact comparisons, analytical scaling families, licensed building-derived controlled inputs and semantic controls exposing erroneous simplifications.
\end{enumerate}
These are scoped method and evaluation contributions. Shared-cost labelled connectivity and rank-based partition reduction are prior work. A difference from one concrete generic upper bound does not establish first-ever priority or a lower bound on every existing method.

\section{Related Work and Positioning}
\subsection{Architectural Access and Labelled Network Design}
BIM/IFC reasoning already distinguishes adjacency, openings and accessible routes \cite{bim}. Our contract uses that distinction; it does not claim its invention. Minimum labelled connectivity attaches cost to labels shared by edges \cite{labels}; profile-separated bundled activation therefore has an existing network-design interpretation. Heterogeneous graph connectivity makes condition-dependent access explicit \cite{condition}. Profiles or bundles alone do not establish originality.

Architectural generation and geometric repair have different decision contracts. EPSAP evolves multi-level room/opening and vertical-facility geometry with evolutionary and local-search operators \cite{epsap}. PlanFix projects mutable room geometry to reduce violation/displacement energy; its inspected preprint distinguishes energy convergence from feasibility/global optimality and excludes global accessibility routing from the current projector scope \cite{planfix}. Those methods motivate a geometric intervention problem but are not common-class runtime competitors for frozen-polygon, original-action subset optimization. Table~\ref{tab:related} states these scoped correspondences; it does not establish absence of an equivalent prior algorithm.

Conflict-aware Steiner selection studies support and conflict structures \cite{conflicts}. Physical locality alone does not imply a small floor interface. An atomic action with disconnected graph effects also cannot be represented faithfully by a purchase vertex adjacent to every effect endpoint: that vertex invents paths between disconnected supplied components. A correct reduction separates effect occurrences from purchase decisions and retains all paid physical occupancy.

\subsection{Connectivity Compression and the Generic Comparison}
Weighted connectivity partitions admit cut-matrix rank compression and representative families \cite{rank}. We reuse that machinery. The ownership/cell state, complete unit lifetimes, full-action accounting and physical-to-witness completion correspondence are the project transfer.

Weighted distance-neighborhood logic \cite{logic} supplies a concrete generic comparison. Its resource graph has one Usage object per owner/cell, separate Action and supplied-edge Occurrence objects, profile Vertices and Cells. A fixed radius-one, threshold-two formula enforces selected usages, cell capacity one and rooted connectivity via an artificial hub. A supplied linear clique-width expression and membership-weight objective transfer yield
\begin{equation}
T_{\mathrm{rec}}+2^{O(prL+zL+p+zLh)}\poly(B).
\label{eq:generic}
\end{equation}
The transfer uses the source's v2 Theorem 1.2 and weighted definition, through clique-width; it is not the quadratic tree-width entry for connectivity constraints. Appendix~\ref{app:generic} gives the exact formulation. No published generic optimizer is executed in our runtime experiment.

Our displayed owner term is logarithmic rather than linear in $h$. For fixed $h$ the asymptotic interface exponents coincide. A narrower encoding or domain-valued resource/connectivity method might match our dependence. Thus \eqref{eq:generic} compares constructed upper bounds, not a universal separation or priority proof.

\section{Strict Repair Model}
\subsection{Frozen Topology, Profiles and Actions}
An instance has ordered floors, room polygons/identifiers, shared-boundary adjacency $A$, baseline connections, profiles $q\in\{1,\ldots,p\}$, rooted obligations $(s_q,t)$, and original actions $a$. Each profile has one source and any number of targets. Within-floor doors and inter-floor portals induce profile graphs $H_q$ according to active permissions. Layers never mix: a shared price does not permit profile switching along a route.

Strict repair selects complete physical actions $S$, monotonically activating supplied connection bundles. Its objective label is
\begin{equation}
\ell(S)=\left(\sum_{a\in S}c_a,\ |S|,\ \operatorname{sort}(S)\right),
\label{eq:objective}
\end{equation}
ordered lexicographically. Nonnegative original costs use exact decimal arithmetic on JSON numeric representations. Activation is idempotent; each selected original action is charged once. Final polygons, identifiers and $A$ equal the baseline.

Candidate doors, dependencies, declared exclusions, shape changes and metadata-only corrections are outside this solver. Existing baseline doors are permitted. Relaxed topology-changing repair is a separate problem, not mixed into our theorem/evaluation.

\subsection{Geometry and Owner Identity}
The kernel checks footprint/reservation containment, profile width and landing requirements, obstacle avoidance, aligned portal footprints and joint overlap. Room access uses a square-body clearance abstraction. Templates and reservations are supplied evidence or declared experimental assumptions, not inferred from proximity.

Distinct selected portal owners cannot share a collision cell. Same-owner overlap requires global kind, width, physical-profile and per-floor footprint consistency. A selected action occupies its own complete effect footprints, rather than unselected catalogue portions of its owner. Conversely, an irrelevant paid graph effect never loses its footprint.

This fixed two-dimensional model does not certify slab openings, headroom, structure, moving equipment, operational lifts or building-code compliance. Geometry uses the recorded GEOS version and tolerance $10^{-8}$, not exact symbolic construction predicates.

\section{Recognition and the Owned Interface}
Contract baseline within-floor access separately per floor/profile. The implementation conservatively retains all components. Rectangular arrangements produce collision cells above the tolerance, with checked equivalence to the kernel's pairwise conflicts. Tiny arrangement cells outside that contract are conservatively unsupported.

The bipartite incidence graph of available actions and owners defines complete coupling units $J$. Shared owners join actions; collision edges between units do not merge them. Unit support contains every effect/footprint floor of every available action in the unit. Action-by-action span alone is insufficient.

\section{Algorithm and Correctness}
The pipeline is shown in Fig.~\ref{fig:pipeline}. Sweep floors with an $L$-floor look-ahead window and permanent virtual roots, one per profile. A state contains live cell status, selected boundary vertices and profile connectivity partitions. During a unit, a cell is empty, externally occupied or owned by an allowed owner of that unit. After the complete unit, owner labels become occupied bits. Labels retain \eqref{eq:objective} and complete original identifiers.

\begin{figure*}[t]
\centering\includegraphics[width=.97\textwidth]{figures/pipeline.pdf}
\caption{Implemented pipeline. Recognition checks geometry/cell equivalence and complete units. The resource state retains all paid effects; representative reduction acts only within equal occupancy and selected boundary sets. Complete original actions are materialized and replayed before returning an optimal result.}

\subsection{Transitions}
Introduce window vertices and mark targets selected. Attach each actual source to its permanent root. At their earliest endpoint floor, baseline edges branch on witness inclusion without changing baseline materialization. At a unit's first support floor, process every original action once: skip, or take it, pay its full cost and fill all actual effect cells. Reject external/different-owner occupancy. For each taken action, choose subsets of complete supplied connected components separately by profile and glue their witness partitions.

After all unit actions, erase owners to bits. After incident unit/baseline events, forget the floor. Reject a selected non-root block when its last live vertex disappears; drop expired cells. Accept only rooted target witnesses. Replay selected original actions through geometry, adjacency and reachability before returning the minimum accepting label.

\subsection{Conditional Guarantee and State Size}
The proof has four ingredients: identity-dependent reuse stays inside a complete unit; thin witnesses preserve complete repairs; occupancy/partitions suffice for a fixed future continuation; and a minimum-label product cut-row basis preserves the best accepting completion. Appendix~\ref{app:proof} proves these statements, including label order and forgetting.

\begin{theorem}[Recognized-model working guarantee]
For rooted independent fixed portal activations with consistent owners, checked collision-cell equivalence and complete support bound $L$, the unlimited-state algorithm returns the optimum strict original-action label. Its proposed bit-complexity bound is
\begin{equation}
T_{\mathrm{rec}}+2^{O(prL+zL\log(h+2))}\poly(B).
\label{eq:bound}
\end{equation}
\end{theorem}
This is a project correctness argument for the represented model; independent theorem review and priority remain unconfirmed. Inside a unit the resource factor is at most $\prod_c(h(J,c)+2)\le(h+2)^{zL}$. Between units it is $2^{zL}$. Summing representative limits over optional boundary sets gives $3^{prL}$. Witness branching and cut elimination add $2^{O(prL)}$ work; exact costs/identifiers add polynomial bit work. The exponent is an interface encoding, not the plain sum $p+r+z+L+h$.

The implementation caps raw states at 100,000 and cut columns at 65,536. Limit errors are operational failures, not infeasibility or a claim that no unexplored optimum exists.

\section{Experimental Design}
\subsection{Questions and Comparators}
We evaluate (Q1) exact semantic agreement on small inputs; (Q2) catalogue/owner scaling under measured interfaces; (Q3) compression benefit/overhead and operational boundaries; and (Q4) an auditable building-derived controlled adaptation. Four semantic controls test why geometry, ownership and original prices cannot be simplified silently.

Compressed/uncompressed modes solve the same recognized class. The oracle enumerates every subset on at most eight actions, with certificate replay through separate union-find/re-enumeration. Geometry/schema predicates are shared. Larger dense inputs use an analytical lower bound/witness rather than exhaustive certificates. The resource-logic evaluator checks an encoding, not a published optimizer runtime. Earlier shaft/partition solvers have narrower subclasses and are not treated as common-class competitors.

\subsection{Frozen Recipes and Measurements}
The 37 authored cases cover shared assets, partial footprints, atomic bundles, unused paid geometry, profile separation, cyclic witnesses, multiple rooms/destinations, owner multiplicities 2/3/4/8/16, dense candidate counts 8/32/64/128, seeds 0--5 crossed with (edges,profiles)=(2,1)/(4,2)/(6,3), and state-cap/dependency/tiny-cell controls. Parameters vary jointly; this is not a full factorial study isolating every parameter.

Three fresh-process samples are recorded per recognition/DP mode, and one oracle sample per eligible input. Wall time starts inside the worker after imports. Solver times include internal recognition and replay. Separate recognition timing is never subtracted to fabricate DP-only time. Process timeout is 60 seconds. Local timings are descriptive, not isolated hardware significance tests.

Each measurement stores seed, canonical input/source hashes, settings, clean revision, Python/packages/GEOS, machine and status. Authored cases yield 362 manifests; building variants yield 80. Small comparisons have 29+8 oracle certificates, plus the demo certificate: 38 total. Successful comparable status/cost/original-ID labels agree exactly. Unsupported/limit/timeout states are not recoded as infeasibility. Appendix~\ref{app:coverage} preserves full coverage.

\section{Discussion and Threats to Validity}
\subsection{Meaning of the Contribution}
The central result preserves physical/action semantics under a compact owned interface and rooted witness reduction. Semantic controls make that preservation observable. A raw-catalogue-independent exponential term is useful on the analytical families, but polynomial recognition remains substantial and measured parameters can grow. The comparison with \eqref{eq:generic} is parameter accounting rather than empirical superiority over a published implementation.

\subsection{Limits of Evidence}
Correctness evidence is finite and the formal argument still needs independent review. Certificate graph/replay logic is separate, but schema/geometry are shared, so common geometry errors remain possible. Numerical recognition can reject small cells. Arbitrary demands, candidate doors, implications/exclusions, selection-dependent obstruction and independent-background metadata corrections are excluded. Large analytical inputs lack exhaustive optimum certificates.

Only three local timing repetitions are available; ratios can reflect workstation variability. Compression helps little on this corpus and can fail its cut-column cap where full mode succeeds. Generators do not isolate every parameter independently. One licensed building example with hypothetical annotations cannot establish actual renovations, costs or generality across buildings. No restoration reference or human accessibility study is supplied.

Originality remains unresolved: labelled connectivity, rank-based methods and weighted logic cover important ingredients, and a tighter prior resource/connectivity formulation might match the owner dependence. We claim neither first-ever repair nor invention of weighted bases/tensoring, universal superiority, acceptance or a generic lower bound. IEEE formatting cannot remove this scientific uncertainty.

\section{Conclusion}
We presented exact strict repair that keeps frozen room topology and complete original action accounting while compressing profile-specific route witnesses. Owned cells and complete units preserve reuse/collisions, and the conditional bound exposes a measured interface rather than raw identities. Controlled comparisons, explicit semantic failures and licensed building-derived experiments provide reproducible evidence with physical and operational limits intact. The supported contribution, provenance and limitations must survive future venue-specific shortening.
